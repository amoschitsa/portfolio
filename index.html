    1. "What is the difference between Class 1 and Class 2, and which should I do first?"

    This is the most frequent query for new entrants. In the UK, the categories are officially called Category C and Category CE. Class 2 (Category C): Allows you to drive "Rigid" lorries (the cab and trailer are one fixed unit). These are typically used for local deliveries. +1 Class 1 (Category CE): Allows you to drive "Articulated" lorries (the cab and trailer are separate, connected by a pivot). These are used for long-haul and pay significantly more. +1 The 2026 Reality: Since late 2021, UK law allows you to go straight to Class 1 without passing the Class 2 test first. Many platforms now offer "Fast Track" bundles for this.
